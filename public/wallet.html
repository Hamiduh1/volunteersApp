What SoftSolutionsTech Wallet does

SoftSolutionsTech Wallet is the international money transfer service operated by SoftSolutions Technologies LLC. It supports sender onboarding, beneficiary setup, compliance screening, transfer initiation, and payout processing.

Mobile money payouts Used where supported by the beneficiary country, partner coverage, and corridor rules.
Bank transfer payouts Available when bank destination details can be verified and the corridor is open.
Wallet credit options Used where partner integrations support direct value delivery into an approved wallet.
Selected corridors only Availability depends on country, currency, partner coverage, and compliance requirements.

Compliance and safety

SoftSolutionsTech Wallet applies risk and compliance controls as part of transfer processing. Some transfers may require enhanced verification before payout, depending on destination, value, and regulatory triggers.

KYC and KYB checks Identity and business verification where applicable.
AML/CFT screening Checks aligned with anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing controls.
Sanctions review Prohibited party and sanctions screening before payout is released.
Ongoing monitoring Transaction review, record retention, and audit controls required by law or partner policy.
Final processing times vary by corridor, payout method, partner status, and whether additional review is required before payout.

Important disclosures

Transfer services are subject to eligibility, jurisdictional limits, partner availability, and legal or regulatory obligations. SoftSolutionsTech Wallet may decline, delay, or cancel a transaction where required by law, risk policy, or partner constraints.
