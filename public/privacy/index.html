Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Volunteers App collects, uses, and protects information needed to power community activity, events, messaging, wallet tools, support, and role-based features.

Last updated: April 9, 2026 SoftSolutions Technologies LLC

What We Collect

We may collect profile details, contact information, account records, uploaded media, activity history, support requests, device information, and transaction-related metadata needed to operate the app.

Depending on which features you use, data may also include event participation, community interactions, messaging activity, live session participation, moderation records, and wallet-related activity logs.

Permissions

  • Notifications for alerts, reminders, and account updates.
  • Location for nearby marketplace and local community features.
  • Camera and microphone for live sessions and media sharing.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and secure account access.
  • Support community, event, marketplace, and messaging flows.
  • Operate wallet-related tools and customer support.
  • Improve safety, reliability, and service quality.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted service providers for authentication, storage, messaging, notifications, analytics, payments, and live communications. These providers process data only to support core app functionality.

Data Sales And Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when needed to deliver app functionality, respond to support requests, protect users, investigate abuse, or comply with applicable law.
