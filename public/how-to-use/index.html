How To Use Volunteers App

This guide gives users a simple path for getting started with account setup, profile completion, core app areas, wallet-related tools, and safety/support resources.

For users, reviewers, and support teams Updated: April 28, 2026

1. Create Your Account

  1. Sign up with accurate personal details.
  2. Verify your email and, where requested, your phone number.
  3. Use current information so support and account recovery stay reliable.

2. Complete Your Profile

Add the details relevant to your role, such as volunteer, employer, organizer, or other supported profile information. Keep profile information clear, lawful, and up to date.

3. Explore Main Features

  • community posts and social activity
  • jobs, applications, and hiring workflows
  • events, organizer tools, and participation
  • messaging, support, and live experiences

4. Use Wallet Tools Carefully

Add only payment methods you control. Availability of deposit, withdrawal, and transfer features may depend on your location, verification status, provider configuration, and partner support.

5. Follow Safety Rules

Do not share misleading, abusive, prohibited, or fraudulent content. Use reporting and moderation tools responsibly if you see suspicious behavior, abuse, or unsafe activity.

6. Get Help When Needed

The fastest support path is to include your account email, device type, app version, a short problem summary, and screenshots if available.
