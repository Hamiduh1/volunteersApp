Account Responsibility

By using Volunteers App, users agree to provide accurate account information and keep profile, contact, and verification details up to date when using community, event, employment, messaging, and wallet-related features.

Users are responsible for activity performed through their own accounts and should protect their credentials, devices, and authentication methods.

Role-Based Participation

Organizers, employers, volunteers, and other users must use the platform lawfully and honestly. Organizers and employers are responsible for clear requirements, lawful postings, fair review decisions, and accurate event or opportunity information.

Community & Content Rules

  • Do not misuse messaging, live features, wallet tools, or reporting systems.
  • Respect other users across social, marketplace, event, and dating-related areas of the app.
  • Use moderation, support, and safety tools responsibly and in good faith.

Where available, wallet, transfer, deposit, or payout features must be used only for legitimate activity and in accordance with supported corridors, partner requirements, and applicable law.

Some financial features rely on third-party providers or licensed partners. Availability may vary by user, region, verification status, and provider configuration.

Platform Enforcement

The platform may suspend accounts, remove content, limit features, reverse access, or deny service when needed to address abuse, fraud, security incidents, compliance concerns, safety risks, or violations of service policies or applicable law.
