Account Deletion Request

Last updated: April 13, 2026

If you would like to delete your Volunteers App account and associated personal data, you can request deletion by contacting our support team.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@softsolutionstech.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Volunteers App Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address, phone number, or username associated with your account.
  4. Our team may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deletion is processed.
Support website: https://softsolutionstech.com

What data is deleted

  • Account profile details such as your name, username, profile photo, and contact details tied to the app account.
  • User-generated profile content that is not required for legal, security, or financial compliance purposes.
  • Associated app access and authentication records that are no longer required to keep the account active.

What data may be retained

  • Wallet, payment, transfer, payout, fraud-prevention, sanctions-screening, dispute, and reconciliation records that must be retained for legal, tax, financial, compliance, or security reasons.
  • Records needed to investigate abuse, enforce platform rules, prevent fraud, or comply with lawful requests.
  • Limited backup or log data for a short period while deletion requests finish processing.

Retention period

Where retention is required for legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, anti-fraud, or anti-money-laundering reasons, certain records may be retained for up to 7 years or for a longer period if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Partial data deletion

At this time, requests are handled as account-deletion requests. If you need help with a specific privacy concern, contact support@softsolutionstech.com.

For more information about how Volunteers App handles personal information, please review the Privacy Policy.
