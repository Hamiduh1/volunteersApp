## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Terrorist Financing (CFT), and sanctions compliance framework for SoftSolutions Technologies LLC ("Company") for its wallet, transfer, and payout services.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All products and services offered by the Company.
- All customers, beneficiaries, and transactions processed through Company systems.
- All staff, contractors, and third parties engaged in compliance-relevant functions.

## 3. Regulatory Basis
The Company operates a risk-based AML/CFT program aligned to applicable requirements, including:
- U.S. BSA/AML obligations applicable to MSBs.
- Sanctions obligations relevant to jurisdictions where the Company operates.
- Partner and payout-provider compliance requirements.

## 4. Governance and Accountability
- The Company designates an MLRO with responsibility for AML/CFT operations, escalation, and reporting.
- As the Company is in an early operating stage, governance approval is provided by Governing Management / Managing Member.
- Compliance accountability remains with the Company, including where third-party providers are used.

## 5. Risk-Based AML/CFT Framework
The Company applies a risk-based approach across onboarding, transaction processing, and ongoing monitoring.

### 5.1 Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
The Company collects and verifies customer information proportionate to risk, including:
- Identity and contact information.
- Purpose and expected account activity.
- Source of funds indicators where required.

### 5.2 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
EDD applies to higher-risk customers, corridors, or transaction patterns, including:
- Higher-risk geographies.
- Elevated transaction volume or atypical behavior.
- Potential sanctions/PEP/adverse-media concerns.

### 5.3 Sanctions, PEP, and Adverse Media Screening
The Company uses internal controls and third-party screening services, including **Dilisense API**, for name screening and risk flagging.
- Screening occurs at onboarding and/or before sensitive payout flows.
- Positive or potential matches are reviewed before completion.
- Matched or unresolved high-risk cases are blocked, rejected, or escalated.

### 5.4 Beneficiary Verification Controls
The Company maintains beneficiary verification controls, including:
- Name/phone/network/country validation logic.
- Verification outcomes persisted for auditability.
- Verification gating before applicable payout execution.

### 5.5 Transaction Monitoring
The Company monitors transaction activity using system rules, event logs, and manual review for anomalies such as:
- Structuring patterns.
- Velocity spikes.
- Unusual sender/beneficiary behavior.

### 5.6 Suspicious Activity Escalation and Reporting
- Alerts are reviewed by the MLRO.
- Cases with credible suspicion are documented and escalated.
- Reports are filed with competent authorities where legally required.

## 6. Prohibited/Restricted Activity
The Company prohibits:
- Anonymous or fictitious accounts.
- Transactions involving sanctioned parties or prohibited jurisdictions.
- Use of the platform for illegal activity, including fraud and terrorist financing.

## 7. Recordkeeping
The Company retains relevant compliance records, including:
- KYC/CDD/EDD documentation.
- Screening and verification results.
- Transaction logs and case review notes.
- Escalation and reporting evidence.

Retention periods follow applicable legal and partner requirements.

## 8. Training
- AML/CFT awareness is mandatory for personnel performing compliance-sensitive functions.
- Training is documented and updated when controls or regulatory expectations materially change.

## 9. Independent Review
- The Company will conduct periodic independent AML/CFT review/testing.
- Where internal segregation is limited, external review support may be engaged.

## 10. Policy Review Cycle
- This policy is reviewed at least annually, or earlier upon major product, risk, or regulatory change.
- Revisions require Governing Management / Managing Member approval.
